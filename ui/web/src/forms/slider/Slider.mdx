## Track and ranges

Tracks use diamond endpoint caps from the shared <a href="./?path=/docs/design-system-sizing--docs" target={"\_top"} style={{ textDecoration: 'underline' }}>Sizing policy</a>. The left cap follows the filled segment; the right follows the unfilled segment.

Optional, non-interactive range underlays sit behind the track, extending 1 px above and below it, with matching diamond caps and the Slider's color.

## Variant guidelines

Shades are light / dark. The filled track uses `color`, switching to `highlightColor ?? color` during interaction or persistent highlighting. The unfilled track and range underlays keep `color` and the same shade in every state.

The thumb follows the corresponding Appearance variant and shared <a href="./?path=/docs/design-system-appearance--docs#active-glow" target={"\_top"} style={{ textDecoration: 'underline' }}>glow types</a>.
